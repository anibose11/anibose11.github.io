Selected projects

I design studies and build analysis workflows for health data. Each project includes the findings, methods and code.

01Insurance continuity

Survey analysis of monthly insurance coverage, prescription use and spending. Year-end coverage missed gaps earlier in the year.

02Trial results audit

A PostgreSQL warehouse of 2,469 clinical trials and an audit of 1,961, tracing which participant count belongs with each reported result.

03Evidence model audit

A benchmark of local language models on 1,209 trial questions, separating accurate quotations from correct answers.

04Diabetes definition audit

A comparison of diabetes definitions in NHANES, with survey weighting and a checker for mismatched denominators.

05Medication identity

A pipeline from DailyMed labels to FHIR medication records, tested for information loss and terminology mismatches.

06Prescribing suppression

An audit of Medicare prescribing data showing how suppressed records affect drug totals and state comparisons.

07Hospital comparability

An interactive check of hospital rankings that tests comparability and shows the uncertainty behind each ordering.

08Diabetes outreach priorities

A county planning analysis comparing diabetes prevalence, estimated burden and poverty-based outreach priorities.

09Adverse event robustness

An analysis of FDA adverse-event reports showing how case definitions and processing choices change reported associations.

10Retraction-aware evidence

A reproducible audit of 1,000 reviews, linking references to retraction, correction and expression-of-concern records.
