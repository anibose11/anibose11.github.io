JANUMET (SITAGLIPTIN and METFORMIN HYDROCHLORIDE), product NDC 0006-0575

Merck Sharp & Dohme LLC. SPL set d19c7ed0-ad5c-426e-b2df-722508f97d67, version 61 (current), held-out split. Drug-label and terminology data; no patient records.

A two-ingredient tablet whose sitagliptin strength is stated for the active moiety, sold in nested packages. FHIR plus the sidecar keeps every critical attribute; FHIR core alone reads the sitagliptin strength as if it were the phosphate salt, the flat export keeps one ingredient, and the DailyMed packaging service attaches both of the label's metformin strengths to this product.
